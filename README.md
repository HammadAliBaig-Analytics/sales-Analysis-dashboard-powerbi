# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive 4-page Power BI dashboard analyzing 9K sales deals worth $10M in won revenue, covering win rate, revenue growth, and performance by product, agent, manager and region.

## Business Problem
Sales leaders need to know where revenue comes from, who is performing, and where deals are being lost.

## Dataset
- Sales pipeline data (Oct 2016 – Dec 2017): deals, deal stages, products, sales agents, managers, regional offices
- Cleaned and modeled in Power Query

## Dashboard Pages
| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Executive Dashboard | KPIs, deal funnel, revenue by product, agent and month |
| Sales Performance | Revenue by manager and region, win rate by agent, deal stage table |
| Sales Agent Details | Revenue by product series, monthly and yearly trend |
| Product Performance | Revenue by product over time, win rate and deal size table |

## Key Insights
- $10M won revenue, 18.11% revenue growth, 48.16% win rate
- GTX Plus Pro and MG Advanced drive about 48% of revenue
- The GTX series is 59% of sales
- The top agent closed $1.2M, more than double the next best

## Recommendations
1. Turn the top agent's approach into a team playbook
2. Focus on the best-selling product lines and review the weakest
3. Analyze the 2K lost deals by agent and product

Power BI Desktop · Power Query · DAX
